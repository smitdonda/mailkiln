### ⚠ Three to avoid despite being free

- **`forgedpost` / `forgedletter`** — in email, "forged" means *spoofed sender*. Naming a
  deliverability-focused tool after email fraud is an own goal. Drop both.
- **`obsidianpost`** — Obsidian is a very well-known note-taking app. Avoidable confusion in
  an adjacent developer-tools space.

### Verdict on the strong set

**`anvilquill`** is the strongest name found anywhere in this document. It is unambiguous to
spell, three clean syllables, and the two images map exactly onto what the package does:
the anvil builds, the quill writes — a visual editor that ejects real code. Free on npm and
all four TLDs. Tailwind prefix `mf-` → `aq-`.

**`hewnmail`** is punchier and shorter, and "hewn" carries real craft weight. One risk:
people who hear it before seeing it may not land the spelling.

If pronounceability matters more than force, **`mailkiln`** from the previous section still
edges both.

## Notes On The Top Picks

**`mailwright`** — `-wright` means *one who builds* (shipwright, wheelwright, playwright),
which is literally what the package is. Carries the craft connotation behind "ejects
readable JSX instead of an opaque blob". No commercial brand on it in the email space.
Tailwind prefix would move `mf-` → `mw-`. **Domain caveat:** `.com`, `.dev` and `.app` are
all taken — only `.io` and `.sh` are free, which weakens it as a brand.

**`mailloom`** — weaving metaphor fits the lossless import ↔ export round-trip. Softer and
less tool-ish than `mailwright`, and it is the **cleanest name overall once domains are
factored in**: npm free, plus `.dev`, `.app`, `.io` and `.sh` all free. Only `.com` is gone.
Tailwind prefix would move `mf-` → `ml-`.

**`ejectmail`** — leads with the differentiator, but ages into a feature name rather than a
product name.

**On keeping `mailforge`:** the npm name is free, but `Mailforge` is an active commercial
product — Salesforge's cold-email infrastructure offering (their `@salesforge/forge-cli`
and `@salesforge/forge-mcp` packages carry `mailforge` as a keyword alongside
`primeforge` / `warmforge` / `infraforge`). Shipping an email tool under that name means a
brand collision with a funded company in the same category. "Forge" is crowded here
generally. That is the one real argument for renaming before v0.1.0 ships.
